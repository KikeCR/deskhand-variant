# Remote Work Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees whose primary work location is
designated as "remote" in the HR system, as opposed to "in-office" or
"hybrid" employees who are expected on-site.

## Working Hours and Availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during their team's core
collaboration hours (typically 10am-3pm in their local timezone) and to
keep their Slack status accurate. Outside of core hours, remote employees
may structure their day flexibly as long as they meet their commitments.

## Equipment and Expenses

The company ships a laptop and docking station to every remote employee's
home address before their start date. Remote employees may request a
one-time $500 home-office equipment stipend and a $50/month
internet-and-phone reimbursement (see the Benefits FAQ for details). All
company equipment remains company property and must be returned upon
termination of employment.

## Security Requirements

Remote employees must connect to the corporate network via the company VPN
whenever accessing internal systems, keep their endpoint security agent
up to date, and avoid working from unsecured public Wi-Fi networks without
the VPN active. Company laptops must not be shared with other household
members.

## Communication Expectations

Remote employees should default to asynchronous communication (written
updates in Slack or the project tracker) for anything that doesn't need a
live discussion, and should attend their team's daily standup and weekly
1:1 with their manager via video call.

## In-Person Gatherings

Remote employees are expected to travel to a company office at least twice
per year for team offsites; travel and lodging costs are covered by the
company per the travel policy.

## Acknowledgment

Every remote new hire must read this policy and submit an electronic
acknowledgment through the HR portal by the end of their first week.
