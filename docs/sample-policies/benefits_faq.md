# Benefits FAQ

## When do my benefits start?

Health, dental, and vision coverage begin on the first day of the month
following your start date. If you start on the first of a month, coverage
begins immediately.

## How do I enroll?

You have 30 days from your start date to enroll through the benefits
enrollment portal, linked in your welcome email. If you take no action
within 30 days, you will be automatically enrolled in the default PPO plan
with employee-only coverage.

## What retirement plans are available?

Northwind Robotics offers a 401(k) plan with a 4% company match, fully
vested after one year of employment. You can enroll at any time through the
HR portal; contributions begin with your next paycheck after enrollment.

## Do remote employees get any additional benefits?

Yes. Remote employees are eligible for a one-time $500 home-office
equipment stipend (see the IT Setup Checklist) and a $50/month
internet-and-phone reimbursement, submitted monthly through the expense
system.

## What paid time off do I get?

All full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off (PTO) per year,
plus 10 company holidays. PTO accrual begins on your start date, and you
can use accrued time immediately with manager approval.

## Who do I contact with benefits questions?

Email benefits@northwindrobotics.example or use the #benefits-support
Slack channel. People Ops holds a benefits office hour every Wednesday at
11am for live questions.
