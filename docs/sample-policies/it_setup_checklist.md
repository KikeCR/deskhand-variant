# IT Setup Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist tells IT and new hires exactly what equipment and access
needs to be set up before and during Week 1. The steps differ depending on
whether the new hire is remote or in-office, so read the section that
matches your work location.

## For Remote Employees

Remote new hires should complete the following during Week 1:

- IT ships a company laptop and a docking station to the employee's home
  address at least 3 business days before their start date, via tracked
  courier.
- On Day 1, the employee installs the company VPN client and connects to
  the corporate network to verify access to internal tools.
- The employee submits a one-time home-office equipment stipend request
  (up to $500) through the expense system for a monitor, chair, or desk.
- The employee reads and electronically acknowledges the Remote Work Policy
  (see the separate Remote Work Policy document) before the end of Week 1.
- IT schedules a 15-minute remote equipment check-in call for Day 2 to
  confirm the laptop, VPN, and video conferencing hardware all work
  correctly.

## For In-Office Employees

In-office new hires should complete the following during Week 1:

- The front desk issues a physical badge and access card on Day 1, granting
  entry to the building and the employee's assigned floor.
- Facilities assigns a desk and, if requested, a locker, before the
  employee's start date.
- The employee visits the on-site IT help desk during Day 1 to pick up
  their laptop, monitor, and peripherals, and to get help connecting to the
  office Wi-Fi and printers.
- The employee receives a parking permit or public-transit subsidy card
  from Facilities, depending on their commute preference.
- IT walks the employee through the office's conference room booking system
  and desk-reservation app (for hybrid teams).

## Shared Steps (All Employees)

Regardless of location, every new hire must:

- Set a strong password and enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) on
  their company account on Day 1.
- Install the company's endpoint security agent on any device used for
  work.
- Complete the security awareness training module within the first 5
  business days.

## Escalation

If equipment does not arrive or accounts are not provisioned by the
employee's start date, the hiring manager should contact the IT Help Desk
directly rather than waiting for the standard ticket queue.
