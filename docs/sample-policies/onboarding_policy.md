# New Hire Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the standard onboarding process for every new hire at
Northwind Robotics, regardless of department or work location. It exists so
that new employees have a consistent, predictable first two weeks and so
that managers, IT, and People Ops know exactly what is expected of them.

## Before Day 1

People Ops sends the new hire a welcome email with their start date, the
name of their onboarding buddy, and a link to the benefits enrollment
portal. The hiring manager schedules a 30-minute welcome call for the
morning of Day 1 and ensures the new hire's accounts (email, Slack, HR
system) are provisioned at least two business days before their start date.

## Week 1: Orientation

Every new hire attends a company-wide orientation session on Day 1, covering
company history, mission, org structure, and a walkthrough of internal
tools (Slack, the HR portal, and the expense system). By the end of Week 1,
every new hire should have:

- Met their manager and onboarding buddy
- Completed all mandatory compliance training (code of conduct, security
  awareness, harassment prevention)
- Reviewed and signed their offer letter and any role-specific agreements
- Set up their core accounts and workstation (see the IT Setup Checklist
  for exact steps, which differ for remote and in-office hires)

## Week 2: Role Ramp-Up

Starting in Week 2, onboarding responsibility shifts from People Ops to the
hiring manager and team. The new hire's manager assigns a 30-60-90 day plan
with concrete goals. Department-specific tool access (engineering systems,
sales systems, etc.) should already be provisioned by the end of Week 1 so
that Week 2 can focus on real work rather than access requests.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **People Ops**: account provisioning, benefits enrollment, compliance
  training assignment, and scheduling the Week 1 orientation.
- **Hiring Manager**: welcome call, onboarding buddy assignment, 30-60-90
  day plan, and day-to-day ramp-up support.
- **IT**: equipment provisioning and account setup, differing by location
  (see the IT Setup Checklist).
- **New Hire**: complete compliance training, review policies, and attend
  scheduled onboarding sessions.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by People Ops and updated as needed to
reflect changes in company structure or applicable law.
